About Syntrix Technologies

A Kenyan ICT partner delivering practical technology services and systems.

Syntrix Technologies provides IT services, technology solutions, systems development, infrastructure support, and ICT supplies to organizations across different sectors.

We help businesses, NGOs, hospitals, educational institutions, government agencies, and other organizations use technology to improve efficiency, streamline operations, enhance service delivery, and support sustainable growth.

Vision

Trusted technology partner in Africa

To become a trusted technology partner in Africa by delivering innovative, reliable, and accessible IT solutions that transform organizations and communities.

Mission

Practical technology for better service delivery

To empower organizations through professional IT services and innovative systems that improve efficiency, productivity, and service delivery.

Commitment

Long-term technology partnerships

From computer maintenance to organization-wide systems, networks, and digital transformation projects, we deliver practical, scalable, and cost-effective solutions.

Core values

The standards behind our service.

  • Reliability: delivering dependable technology, services, and support.
